---
name: Reseller credit policy
description: Non-expiring prepaid credits and the required financial history policy.
---

Reseller credits remain in the account until used. Buying another package adds to
the existing balance; it is not a recurring allowance or a balance reset.

Correct balances with recorded adjustments rather than deleting financial history.

**Why:** The user explicitly required non-expiring, accumulating credits and a
durable financial audit trail.

**How to apply:** Preserve these rules when adding billing changes, account
maintenance, statements, refunds or data-cleanup features.
